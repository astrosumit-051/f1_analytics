# Introduction

An undercut is the attempt to pass a rival through the pit stops: the car behind
stops first, uses its fresh tyres for a fast out-lap, and hopes to be ahead when the
rival comes out of its own stop. Pit walls decide this in seconds. We ask a narrow
question the data can answer: **for a car a given number of seconds behind a rival,
how likely is the undercut to put it ahead and keep it ahead for three laps, and how
does that depend on how long the rival waits to respond?**

The answer is reported as a probability curve over the starting gap, the *undercut
window*, with the gaps at which the chance falls below 50% and 25%. The project
follows the revised proposal (revision 3, `Proposal/F1_Undercut_Window_Proposal_v2.docx`)
and the decisions in `DECISIONS.md` (D-001 to D-010).
