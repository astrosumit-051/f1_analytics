Undercut Lab / Monte Carlo

You pit first. The car ahead stays out for one, two or three laps. Will you still be ahead three laps after both stops? Here the frozen model is turned into a simulator: every scenario is played out thousands of times over real 2018–2023 race situations.

How it works

Assumptions

  • The model is taken as the truth: final_v1 (gap spline, delay group, tyres, pit lane, rejoin traffic, cars between, overtaking, recent pace, track temperature) plus the pit-lane excess term from extension 1, fitted on the core training pairs.
  • A slow stop costs the same at every gap and delay, and changes nothing else. Pit-lane excess is clipped to −2 to 10 s.
  • The other race features are drawn from real training pit cycles independently of the gap. Delays of 2 and 3 laps share one group, as in the model.
  • The outcome is success over 3 laps judged at the common lap (chaser stop + 4), so every delay is compared at the same point in the race.
  • No 2024, 2025 or 2026 outcome is used anywhere on this page.
